    craftRecipe RefillSpiritInBeerBottle3

    /* ----------------------------------------------------------------------
       BACKLOG 2.8 (2026-09-06) -- bucket decanting, revived.

       Identical in shape to RefillSpiritIn{Whiskey,Beer,Wine,...}Bottle3/4/5:
       ONE serving of the pot (bare `item 1` on a base:drainable = one use,
       UseDelta 0.25/0.16/0.125 -> 4/6/8 per pot) for one filled vessel, and
       NO OnCreate -- the pot's own ReplaceOnDeplete hands the empty
       Moonshine.DistillPot* back when the last serving is drawn.

       `flags[ItemCount]` on the bucket is LOAD-BEARING and is the whole reason
       the 2026-08-26 attempt (acef84b) was reverted.  Base.BucketEmpty is a
       real `component FluidContainer`, and quirks rule 65 says such an input
       is COUNTED but NEVER CONSUMED without the flag.  Measured A/B
       (tools/pzrepl/tests/bucketdecant.lua, probes on):
           item 1 [Base.BucketEmpty]                    bucket 1->1  spirit +1
           item 1 [Base.BucketEmpty] flags[ItemCount]   bucket 1->0  spirit +1
       Without the flag this is an infinite spirit mint.

       The reverted version ALSO carried `OnCreate = GiveDistillSmallEmpty` on
       each of these (the bottle family does not).  Measured: that hands out a
       free empty still pot on EVERY craft -- 4 free Distill Pot I per pot.
       Deliberately absent here.

       Input is Base.BucketEmpty ONLY, not a wide bucket bracket: the output
       is a single fixed item whose ReplaceOnUse gives a Base.BucketEmpty back,
       so accepting Base.BucketWood / Base.BucketForged / Base.BucketCarved
       would launder a wooden or carved bucket into a metal one.  (Widening
       Base.BucketEmpty -> +Base.Bucket is a separate, mod-wide question: the
       mash chain and FillDistillWithWater name BucketEmpty only too.)
       ---------------------------------------------------------------------- */

    /* ----------------------------------------------------------------------
       BACKLOG 2.8 (2026-09-06) -- the water half of bucket decanting.

       Same F11/U8 idea as the 11 RefillWaterIn* recipes above -- keep the
       player's OWN vessel (`mode:keep`) and fill it in place with REAL vanilla

       serving, and add exactly the litres that pot is worth (4 / 6 / 8).

       WHY, measured (tools/pzrepl/tests/bucketdecant.lua):
       the shared FillKeptContainerWithWater fills the kept container to its
       CAPACITY.  For a 1 L bottle or a jar that happens to equal the one
       serving it charges.  A bucket is 10 L, so the naive port measured
       **10.00 L out of a single 1 L serving of a 4 L pot**, and the round trip

       The shape below is exploit-free and self-gating:

                                      draw 4 L out of a pot with 1 serving left
