description=[h1]Suggested procedure[/h1]
description=[olist]
description=[*]Collect Distilling Vol.1 and Vol.2, then craft a Distill Pot (I).
description=[*]Craft Mash from potato and corn products.
description=[*]Close the bucket with a tarp and a tie — set it to ferment inside your residence for up to 20 in-game days.
description=[*]Fill the fermented Mash into the Distill Pot.
description=[*]Add the Distill Pot to a heat source appropriate to its size.
description=[*]Observe the cooking process and enjoy the fruits of your labor.
description=[/olist]
description=
description=[h1]Build your still up[/h1]
description=Add a [b]coal filter[/b] to a Distill Pot (I) to unlock the [b]Distill Pot (II)[/b] — this opens up the second distillation for higher-proof spirit, and Isopropyl alcohol for disinfection.
description=
description=Extend the Distill Pot (II) with a [b]temperature controller column[/b] to reach the [b]Distill Pot (III)[/b], which turns Motor Oil into Gasohol: real fuel for vehicles and generators.
description=
description=Build a [b]Gasohol Drum[/b] and manage your gas cans properly, from one big container you can fill from.
description=
description=[h1]Five flavours of White Lightning[/h1]
description=Steep your Appalachian White Lightning with fruit or herbs to take the edge off the raw shine — [b]apple, cherry, peach, lemon and mint[/b]. Easier drinking than the unflavoured stuff, still rougher than anything you'd find in a liquor store.
