    /* BULK RECHARGE: 3 / 4 buckets = the same 6 / 8 L the bottle and jar routes
       demand. flags[ItemCount] is REQUIRED, BucketRefill is base:food.
       docs/script-notes.md sec. 8.

       POT I HAS NO RECHARGE, ON PURPOSE (2026-09-09).  A Pot I recharge existed
       briefly (b571646) and was dropped: Pot II/III recharge into
       Alc_DistillPot{Medium,Large}RefillSpirit, which ReplaceOnCooked into
       isopropyl -- the recharge is the ENTRANCE to the 2nd-distillation track.
       Pot I has no Alc_DistillPotSmallRefillSpirit and no ...Iso (that track is
       deliberately gated behind Pot II), so a Pot I recharge could only hand back
       the ordinary charged pot: spirit in, the same spirit out, no product gained.
       Decanting Pot I to buckets is a one-way trip by design -- upgrade to Pot II
       (UpgradeDistillIToII) to get a recharge that is worth something. */
